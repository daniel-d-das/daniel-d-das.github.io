CohortCal

A shared submission calendar for MBA cohorts, taken from problem definition to a working prototype.

Context
MBA coursework, individual assignment
Output
Report and working prototype
Try the live prototype View the source on GitHub

The problem

At my college, DigiCampus shows classes and sessions on its calendar, but not when assignments and other submissions are due. That leaves students without a calendar view of what they need to hand in, and when.

The idea

CohortCal is one shared submission calendar for a whole cohort. Students join by choosing their institution and programme. The Student Council keeps the calendar up to date, everyone gets a free daily digest, and optional premium features add reminders on top.

What the prototype does

  • Quick sign-up. Three steps: institution, programme (PGPM or PGDM) and name. Great Lakes is the live institution, with IIM Bangalore and ISB Hyderabad shown as coming soon.
  • Free daily digest. A card at the top shows how many submissions are due in the next seven days, and the two that are nearest.
  • Cohort calendar. Submissions are sorted by deadline and colour-coded by subject, with a badge when something is due in two days or less.
  • Student Council mode. A password-protected mode, unlocked with a demo password in this prototype, where council members add and remove subjects and submissions.
  • Premium reminders. A demo switch unlocks custom reminder timing, per-subject reminders, per-submission reminders and DM delivery by WhatsApp or email. The most specific setting wins: a submission's own reminder, then its subject's, then the default.

This is a front-end prototype. It runs in the browser with sample data and saves to that browser only, so reminders are not actually sent and calendars are not yet shared between users.
