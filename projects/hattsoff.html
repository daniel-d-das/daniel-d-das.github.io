Hattsoff Store Marketing

Paid social, ad creative, product designs and promo videos for an online T-shirt and hoodie store.

Context
Work project, online apparel store
Period
2020 and 2024–25
Focus
Meta Ads, creative, product design
Visit the store

The brief

Hattsoff Store sells printed T-shirts and hoodies online. The store needed customers to find it and a steady supply of designs and ads to sell, so the work covered both sides: running the marketing and making the creative.

What I did in 2020

  • Product lines. I designed product lines for the store.
  • Ads. I created the ad creatives and wrote the ad copy, and managed the Meta (Facebook) Ads campaigns.
  • Organic channels. I ran the social media accounts and content, and the SEO for the store's website.
  • Result. The Facebook Ads campaigns generated more than ₹60,000 in revenue within three months of starting.

What I made in 2024–25

In 2024–25 I worked with the store on an ongoing basis, producing further creative: new designs and mockups, square ads with the price and free-shipping offer built into the image, a New Year sale poster, and short promo videos for a plain-colour T-shirt range and a Dosa Idli Sambar Chutney design.

Ad creatives

Each ad shows the product on a hanger over character artwork, with a price flag at the top and the shipping offer and logo at the bottom, so the offer reads at a glance in a feed.
