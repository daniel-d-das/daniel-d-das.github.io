Meesho Order Fulfilment

A BPMN 2.0 model of how a Meesho order moves between the customer, the platform, the seller and the logistics partner, with the business rules behind it and three ideas for automating it.

The problem

Meesho is a zero-commission marketplace that connects resellers and buyers with a large base of unbranded sellers, and it relies on third-party logistics partners for pickup and last-mile delivery. An order therefore passes through several independent parties, which creates visibility gaps, delayed order confirmations, inconsistent packaging checks and many failed first-attempt deliveries, especially for cash-on-delivery orders in tier-2 and tier-3 markets.

The goal was to give management and an ERP implementation team one standard view of the process, so it can be automated in an order management system with clear checkpoints and exception handling.

The model

The diagram follows one order from the moment it is placed on the Meesho app. It uses four swim lanes: the customer, the Meesho platform as a system, the seller, and the logistics partner. Three decision gateways (payment valid, stock available, delivery successful) split the flow into four possible endings: delivered, rejected for invalid payment, cancelled because stock was unavailable, or refunded after a failed delivery.

Business rules

I turned the exceptions into five rules, each with a trigger, an action and a priority, so a system could enforce them. The Excel file also lists the BPMN step, the approving role and the data source for each rule.

RuleWhat it saysPriority
Payment check If a prepaid payment fails or cannot be verified, reject the order and notify the customer within 15 minutes. High
Stock check If the item is out of stock at the seller, cancel the order within 2 hours, refund in full if it was prepaid and notify the customer. High
Seller dispatch deadline The seller must accept, pack and hand over within 48 hours, or the order escalates to Meesho Operations. Medium
Packing quality A package that is damaged, wrong or incomplete must be re-packed before handover and cannot ship until it passes. High
Failed delivery After two failed attempts the logistics partner returns the package to origin, and Meesho refunds the customer within 7 business days of receiving it, with Finance approving the refund. High

What I would improve

  1. Automate stock and payment validation. Connect the order management system to the seller's inventory feed and the payment gateway so both checks happen in real time at checkout, which cuts orders cancelled after checkout.
  2. Generate the shipping label in parallel with packing. Today the label is created after the seller hands the package over. Creating it as soon as the order is forwarded would shorten the time from pickup to dispatch.
  3. Join the two exception paths in one dashboard. Packing failures (handled by sellers) and failed deliveries (handled by logistics partners) are tracked separately. One seller-performance view would flag sellers with high re-pack rates and pin codes with high return-to-origin rates, and support seller coaching or limits on cash on delivery in risky areas.
