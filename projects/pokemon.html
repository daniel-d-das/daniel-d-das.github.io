Pokémon Trading Cards in India

A digital marketing plan for The Pokémon Company's trading card game in the Indian market: who to reach, where to reach them, and what to show them.

Course
Digital Marketing, PGPM
Team
Group project, Group 21
Product and market
Trading cards, India

The problem

The Pokémon Trading Card Game is well known worldwide. This project asks how digital marketing can introduce it to the right audience in India, and what that audience looks like.

My role

Add your role in the group here, for example which deliverables you owned and what you decided.

How we approached it

  1. Media mix. We mapped owned, earned and paid media, each split into traditional and digital channels, using dated examples from Pokémon's own history such as the anime, Pokémon GO, TCG Pocket and the 2026 Super Bowl ad.
  2. Segmentation and targeting. We compared five segmentation methods (demographic, geographic, psychographic, behavioural and webographic), scored three candidate segments on seven criteria, and chose nostalgic collectors aged 25 to 35 in metro India.
  3. Personas and positioning. We wrote ten illustrative personas across five segments and one ideal persona, Rohan the Nostalgic Collector, then a positioning statement set against three competitor groups.
  4. Creative execution. We turned the plan into a landing page with a pack opener, trainer quiz and rupee price guide, a square poster with a QR code to that page, and a 33-second vertical promo video.

What we found

  • Nostalgic collectors aged 25 to 35 in metro India came out on top: the highest spend per order, the lowest price sensitivity and easy digital reach, with the 30th anniversary in 2026 as a timely hook.
  • With no official distributor in India, the market runs through specialty importer stores, Instagram and YouTube creators, and WhatsApp and Discord groups. Trust and access depend on creators and community more than on open marketplaces.
  • Our targeting scores are qualitative, built from retail listings and community research rather than market-size data, so the next step would be a survey to confirm the segment.
