Pokémon Trading Cards in India

A digital marketing plan for The Pokémon Company's trading card game in the Indian market: who to reach, where to reach them, and what to show them.

Course
Digital Marketing, PGPM
Team
Group project, Group 21
Product and market
Trading cards, India

The problem

The Pokémon Trading Card Game is well known worldwide. This project asks how digital marketing can introduce it to the right audience in India, and what that audience looks like.

My role

Add your role in the group here, for example which deliverables you owned and what you decided.

How we approached it

  1. Media mix. We mapped owned, earned and paid media across traditional and digital channels, with how each is applied and a rollout timeline.
  2. Segmentation, targeting and positioning. We compared several segmentation methods, one slide each, then chose a target segment and a positioning for it.
  3. Customer persona. We described the target customer in a persona worksheet, so every later choice could be checked against one person.
  4. Creative execution. We turned the plan into a landing page, a poster with a QR code to that page, and an animated promo video that opens a nostalgic card binder and ends on a pack pull.
